# About surface brief

Mode: Persuade.

Audience: People deciding what Avenor is, why it exists, and whether the people behind it are credible and approachable.

Job and action: Understand the product, mission, vision, and team; then explore the library or contact the team.

Proof and content: Real Open Library-backed discovery capabilities, existing mission and vision copy, one mentor and six member records with real photographs, and the existing contact/request experience.

Constraints: Preserve the global navbar and footer. Keep the contact area and all mentor/member photographs. Do not invent metrics, awards, customers, or partnerships.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Avenor is a living connection between readers, books, purpose, and the people maintaining it; the page refuses the usual centered About hero followed by interchangeable value cards.

OWN-WORLD: A restrained warm-paper field, deep forest ink, antique-gold connectors, quiet sage, fine catalogue rules, circular nodes, compact labels, and real portrait photography. The physical scene is a bright reading room at midmorning.

STORY: Visitors understand Avenor’s discovery role, see mission and vision as connected commitments, meet the real team, and reach a clear invitation to explore or contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A large left-aligned statement owns the left third. A central Avenor medallion connects across the remaining field to Discovery, Mission, Vision, and People; book covers and small real team portraits prove each branch. “Meet the team” closes the map at lower right.

FORM: Connected-by-Books cultural atlas, ranked seventh in the grounded structure list and selected from surface seed `185f1aaf`.
