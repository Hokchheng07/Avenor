# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to understand what Avenor is, why it exists, what it is trying to accomplish, and who is building it.

## Product Purpose

Avenor is a book-discovery experience that helps people explore books and authors, inspect book details, and keep track of books they care about. The About page must make the product, its mission, its vision, and the people behind it understandable and credible.

## Operating Context

Visitors move between a public homepage, an Open Library-powered discovery catalog, book-detail views, authentication pages, and an About page with team and contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Book search and discovery are backed by Open Library data.
- Login and registration are separate public routes.
- Book details can be saved locally in the browser.
- The About page includes a working contact/request area.
- The existing global navbar and footer must remain in place.
- The About redesign must retain the contact area and the existing mentor and member photographs.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Avenor name and existing logo assets.
- Present Avenor as a thoughtful literary product built by a real team.
- The About experience must clearly cover what Avenor is, its mission, its vision, and the members behind it.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing brand assets under `public/Brand/`.
- Existing team and mentor records in `src/data/team.ts`, with member imagery under `public/Images/`.
- Existing contact, testimonial, and support components under `src/Components/Contact/`.
- Existing About-page copy and contact details in `src/app/about/page.tsx`.
- No external awards, customer counts, usage metrics, press claims, or independently verified testimonials may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the purpose understandable before asking visitors to act.
- Let the real team and real product capabilities provide credibility.
- Keep literary discovery calm, direct, and welcoming.
- Preserve useful functionality while reducing visual and narrative clutter.
